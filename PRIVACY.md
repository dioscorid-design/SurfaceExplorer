# Privacy Policy — Surface Explorer 4D

_Last updated: 2026-06-29_

Surface Explorer 4D ("the app") is a mathematical surface visualizer developed by Gaetano Moschetti.

## Data collection

**Surface Explorer 4D does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- The app has no user accounts and requires no sign-in.
- The app does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other personal information.

## Your content

All presets, scripts, recordings, and other content you create or open remain **on your device**. They are never uploaded to any server and are not accessible to the developer.

## Network access

The app does not require an internet connection to function and does not send your data to any external service.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
