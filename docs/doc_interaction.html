The mouse, the fingers on iPhone and iPad, and a game controller on Mac, iPad and iPhone move the view, the objects of a scene and the camera paths. What every gesture and every button does is shown with pictures in two windows of the Help menu (on iPhone, iPad and Android, in the menu of the button at the top left):

  • Mouse & Touch: dragging, the wheel and the fingers, in the 3D and 4D view (surface, environment, selected object, camera path) and in the 2D texture view.
  • Game Controller: the sticks, the triggers and the buttons. Pair the controller in the system settings (Bluetooth) or connect it with a cable; the status bar says when it connects or disconnects.

On a computer the windows can stay open beside the view while you try the commands.
