Interaction & Navigation

← User Interface  ·  Contents  ·  Projection Modes →

Navigation controls change slightly depending on whether you are viewing a 3D/4D surface or previewing a 2D Texture/Image.

 

3D & 4D Surface Mode

  • Left Mouse Button (Drag): Rotates the surface (or the camera, depending on the mode selected in the UI).
  • Right Mouse Button (Drag): Slides the view sideways: the camera moves without turning, and the centre of the scene stays under the pointer. In orthographic projection the size stays the same. It works on a single surface and, in a scene, with the environment or a fixed object selected; with a free object selected the right button moves the object, and while a camera path runs (or with MOUSE: PATH) it moves the path, as described below. It does nothing during a recording.
  • Mouse Wheel: Zooms in and out.
  • On iPhone and iPad: one finger rotates like the left button, the pinch zooms like the wheel, and moving two fingers together slides the view like the right button, the centre of the scene between the fingers. In a scene with an object selected, moving two fingers together moves the object, its centre between the fingers, while the pinch changes its size.
  • While a camera path runs: the camera belongs to the path, so the mouse reshapes the path itself and writes the change into its fields: drag to turn it, hold Shift while dragging to turn the 4D path toward W, drag with the right button to move it, scroll to make it smaller or larger. With the path stopped, the MOUSE button under DEPARTURE chooses between moving the scene and reshaping the path; RESET beside it brings the path back. See Animation & Video.
  • In a scene: with an object selected, the wheel and the pinch change its size, dragging rotates it and dragging with the right button (on a touch screen, with two fingers) moves it, its centre under the pointer; with the environment selected they move the camera, turn the whole scene and, with the right button, slide it. See Scenes.

 

2D Texture/Image Mode

When viewing the texture preview (Flat View):

  • Left Mouse Button (Drag): Rotates the texture continuously.
  • Right Mouse Button (Drag): Moves the texture, which follows the pointer at any zoom and rotation. This works on the surface, the background and the border texture.
  • On iPhone and iPad: one finger rotates the texture, a tap turns it by 90 degrees, the pinch zooms, and moving two fingers together moves the texture.
  • Mouse Wheel: Zooms into the texture.
  • Left Mouse Button (Single Click): Instantly rotates the image by 90 degrees.
