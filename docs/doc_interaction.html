Interaction & Navigation

Navigation controls change slightly depending on whether you are viewing a 3D/4D surface or previewing a 2D Texture/Image.

 

3D & 4D Surface Mode

  • Left Mouse Button (Drag): Rotates the surface (or the camera, depending on the mode selected in the UI).
  • Mouse Wheel: Zooms in and out.
  • While a camera path runs: the camera belongs to the path, so the mouse reshapes the path itself and writes the change into its fields: drag to turn it, hold Shift while dragging to turn the 4D path toward W, drag with the right button to move it, scroll to make it smaller or larger. With the path stopped, the MOUSE button under DEPARTURE chooses between moving the scene and reshaping the path; RESET beside it brings the path back. See Animation & Video.
  • In a scene: with an object selected, the wheel and the pinch change its size and dragging rotates it; with the environment selected they move the camera and turn the whole scene. See Scenes.

 

2D Texture/Image Mode

When viewing the texture preview (Flat View):

  • Left Mouse Button (Drag): Rotates the texture continuously.
  • Mouse Wheel: Zooms into the texture.
  • Left Mouse Button (Single Click): Instantly rotates the image by 90 degrees.
