Interaction & Navigation

Navigation controls change slightly depending on whether you are viewing a 3D/4D surface or previewing a 2D Texture/Image.

 

3D & 4D Surface Mode

  • Left Mouse Button (Drag): Rotates the surface (or the camera, depending on the mode selected in the UI).
  • Mouse Wheel: Zooms in and out.

 

2D Texture/Image Mode

When viewing the texture preview (Flat View):

  • Left Mouse Button (Drag): Rotates the texture continuously.
  • Mouse Wheel: Zooms into the texture.
  • Left Mouse Button (Single Click): Instantly rotates the image by 90 degrees.
