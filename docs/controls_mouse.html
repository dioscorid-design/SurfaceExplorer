Reshaping the camera path writes it into the path fields; RESET under DEPARTURE brings them back.
With Shift, dragging with the mouse turns the 4D camera path toward W.
While a camera path runs, with a fixed object selected nothing moves. During REC, neither the mouse nor the fingers move paths or objects.

The mouse is a stick: press and move away from that point; the farther you go, the faster the camera turns, and releasing stops the turn.
On iPhone and iPad the sticks appear where you put your thumbs: the left half of the view sets the speed and turns, the right half pitches and rolls. With a keyboard attached the keys work too.
