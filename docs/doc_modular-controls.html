Modular Activation & Master Controls

← Library, Presets & Saving Your Work  ·  Contents  ·  Acknowledgments & Third-Party Code →

Every animated module has its own controls, and one master switch drives them all:

  • Local controls — the Run and Stop buttons of the Equations and Script docks, the Run of the Ray Marching texture, Departure for the camera paths, the Texture checkbox — act only on their own module.
  • START / STOP on the status bar starts or stops every loaded module at once: geometry and its t animation, textures, background, sound, rotations and camera paths.
